# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in FreeScribe welcoming, respectful,
and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and constructive in discussions and reviews;
- welcoming different viewpoints and language communities;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes; and
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
sexualized language or imagery, publishing private information, and other
conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the
contact method listed in the repository. Maintainers may remove comments,
close contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and whenever an individual is
officially representing the project.
