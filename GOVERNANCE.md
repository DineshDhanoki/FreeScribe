# Project governance

FreeScribe is maintained as a community project led by the repository owner.
Contributors are encouraged to propose changes through issues and pull
requests, with decisions documented in the relevant discussion.

## Decision principles

- Protect user privacy and keep audio local by default.
- Prefer evidence from tests, evaluation data, and reproducible benchmarks.
- Treat Indian-language quality as a first-class product concern.
- Keep changes understandable, documented, and accessible to new contributors.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers review pull requests, protect the default branch, publish releases,
respond to security reports, and may request changes when privacy, licensing,
or quality requirements are not met.

## Becoming a maintainer

Regular contributors who demonstrate reliable technical judgment, respectful
collaboration, and sustained project involvement may be invited to help review
and maintain the project.
